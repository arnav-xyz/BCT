Why Blockchain for Mental Health?

  • Pseudonymous Identity: No real names, emails, or phone numbers. Only your cryptographic wallet address.
  • Tamper-Proof History: Daily mood logs have cryptographic timestamps that can't be falsified or altered retroactively.
  • True Data Sovereignty: You hold the keys. Centralized hospitals or insurance companies cannot mine or sell your data.
  • Selective Therapist Access: Grant and revoke read permission to clinical professionals dynamically on-chain.
